Read `references/soldier-runtime.md` completely immediately before building every generation request. ## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one saved memory-mark, not a scene from a picture book. Soft contour, flat naive shapes, a little imperfection. A star, a plant or a short line may sit beside it, but must not grow into a setting. Material is colored pencil and chalky pastel on paper. The mark is small and surrounded by blank. Reject a commercial logo, realistic proportion, a full illustration and smooth vector.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-314/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
